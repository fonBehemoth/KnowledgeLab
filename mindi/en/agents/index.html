Financial analyst, controller and a final report: MINDI’s first sequential specialist workflow.

DIGITAL SPECIALISTS

One task.
More than one perspective.

MINDI’s first financial workflow: an analyst examines materials, a controller reviews the conclusions, and a final report retains the observations.

01

Financial analyst

Studies the materials, compares information and prepares an initial analysis.

02

Controller

Reviews the conclusions and records discrepancies and observations.

03

Final report

Brings the results together, retaining the analysis and observations for review.

Responsibilities, available data and permitted actions are defined separately. The first workflow has been tested locally with prepared responses.

A role means a specific task.
