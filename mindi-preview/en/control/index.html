Control and access

Decisions and permissions
remain with you.

Data access, confirmation of significant changes, and the ability to examine the basis of a result.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL

You define
the permissions.

Decide who can access materials, protect sign-in and review significant events. An AI response does not itself authorise an action.

Server-enforced access

Access boundaries

Company, department, employee. Permission to read a material is separate from permission to use it for analysis.

Action confirmation

Protected changes require confirmation. Under the strict policy, broader permissions require approval.

Activity journal

Significant events, retention policies and archives. Document contents and secrets stay out of the journal.
